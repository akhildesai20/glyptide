Design principles

Motion with a job.

Preserve what exists.

Only incoming or edited content should move. Never animate the whole document again.

Use springs sparingly.

Near-critical damping gives small position changes a natural stop. Text should not bounce or stretch.

Keep the reader in control.

Pause, stop, review, and discard without losing the original document.

Communicate actual state.

Waiting and interruption are explicit; no invented progress percentages.
